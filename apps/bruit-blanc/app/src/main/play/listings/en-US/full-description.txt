Fall asleep faster, soothe your baby and stay focused with a soft, steady background sound.

White Noise offers three sounds, generated live by the app:
• White noise: an even hiss that masks voices, traffic and snoring.
• Pink noise: softer, like steady rain. Great for sleep.
• Brown noise: a deep rumble, like a distant waterfall. Perfect for focus.

• Keeps playing with the screen off and in the background.
• Sleep timer: 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h or 8 h.
• Seamless sound with no gaps or audible loops.
• No data collected, no account, no unnecessary permissions.
• Dark interface designed for night time.

Made for:
• Parents helping their baby fall asleep.
• Light sleepers bothered by noise.
